# Saint Therese Church Website - Design Strategy

## Design Approach: Sacred Minimalism

**Design Movement:** Contemporary Sacred Architecture meets Digital Minimalism
- Inspired by modern church architecture that balances reverence with accessibility
- Clean, breathing layouts that honor the spiritual nature of the content
- Sophisticated use of negative space and light

**Core Principles:**
1. **Reverence through Clarity** - Every element serves a purpose; nothing distracts from the message
2. **Warmth in Simplicity** - Soft, welcoming color palette that feels inviting, not cold
3. **Accessibility as Respect** - Clear hierarchy and navigation honors the diversity of the congregation

**Color Philosophy:**
- **Primary:** Deep stone blue (oklch(0.35 0.08 260)) - evokes trust, tradition, and spiritual calm
- **Secondary:** Warm cream (oklch(0.95 0.01 80)) - welcoming, soft background
- **Accent:** Gold (oklch(0.65 0.15 70)) - subtle elegance, traditional church symbolism
- **Neutral:** Warm grays for text and structure
- Emotional intent: Peaceful, welcoming, trustworthy, timeless

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Full-width hero with background video (modern, engaging)
- Asymmetric sections with generous whitespace
- Card-based staff directory with subtle depth
- Vertical rhythm that guides the eye naturally

**Signature Elements:**
1. **Background Video Hero** - Immersive, sets the tone immediately
2. **Soft Dividers** - Subtle separators between sections (not harsh lines)
3. **Typography Hierarchy** - Large, elegant headings paired with readable body text

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Smooth, purposeful transitions (nothing jarring)
- Hover states that provide gentle feedback
- Modals for additional information (staff bios, event details)

**Animation Guidelines:**
- Entrance animations: 300-400ms ease-out for sections as user scrolls
- Hover effects: 200ms transitions on cards and links
- Video: Auto-play muted, loop continuously
- No excessive motion - respect the sacred nature of the space

**Typography System:**
- **Display Font:** Playfair Display (elegant, traditional, for headings)
- **Body Font:** Inter (modern, readable, for all body text)
- **Hierarchy:** 
  - H1: 3.5rem (Playfair Display, bold)
  - H2: 2.5rem (Playfair Display, bold)
  - H3: 1.5rem (Inter, semibold)
  - Body: 1rem (Inter, regular)

**Brand Essence:**
- **One-liner:** A welcoming digital home for the Saint Therese community, honoring tradition while embracing modern accessibility
- **Personality:** Warm, Trustworthy, Reverent

**Brand Voice:**
- Headlines: Inviting, clear, purposeful
- CTAs: Action-oriented but respectful ("Join Us," "Learn More," "Contact Us")
- Microcopy: Helpful, never condescending
- Example lines:
  - "Welcome to our parish family"
  - "Discover how you can serve"

**Logo Concept:**
- Simple cross with subtle floral element (St. Therese = Little Flower)
- Monochromatic, works at any size
- Placed prominently in header and footer

**Signature Brand Color:**
- Deep Stone Blue (oklch(0.35 0.08 260)) - unmistakably this church's spiritual anchor

## Key Features to Build:
1. **Homepage** with hero video background
2. **About/Staff Page** with parish council and staff directory
3. **Navigation** - clear, accessible header
4. **Mass Schedule** - prominent on homepage
5. **Contact Section** - easy access to church info
6. **Responsive Design** - mobile-first approach

## Style Decisions

The Saint Therese identity will use a restrained **cross-and-little-flower language** in the wordmark, section markers, dividers, portrait empty states, and devotional accents. The motif should feel pastoral and reverent rather than decorative or ornate.

Interior and information-dense pages will follow the Resources page’s **editorial sacred hierarchy**: large Playfair headlines, small gold uppercase devotional labels, warm cream negative space, and asymmetric content blocks before dense information.

Staff imagery will remain dignified and pastoral. Every portrait uses a 2:3 frame, while missing portraits use a branded Little Flower treatment instead of generic gray placeholders.
