# Verified Catholic Resource Findings

## Official Scripture and Church Teaching

The [USCCB Bible](https://bible.usccb.org/) provides daily readings, a readings calendar, books of the Bible, study materials, audio podcasts, videos, and email subscriptions. Useful direct links include [Daily Readings](https://bible.usccb.org/), [Books of the Bible](https://bible.usccb.org/bible), and [Daily Readings Audio](https://bible.usccb.org/podcasts/audio).

The official online [Catechism of the Catholic Church](https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/_INDEX.HTM) is available through the Holy See. The [Diocese of Rapid City](https://www.rapidcitydiocese.org/) should be included as the primary regional Catholic link.

## Podcasts and Guided Formation

The official [Ascension podcast catalog](https://app.ascensionpress.com/podcasts) currently includes The Bible in a Year, The Catechism in a Year, The Rosary in a Year, Sunday Homilies, Luke in 50 Days, Ask Fr. Josh, Catholic Classics, and other Catholic formation series. The Resources page should feature The Bible in a Year, The Catechism in a Year, and The Rosary in a Year as approachable starting points.

## Planned Categories

The Resources page will organize links under Scripture & Study, Prayer & Sacraments, Podcasts & Audio, Catholic Teaching, and Local Church.

## Free Study and Formation

[Agape Bible Study](https://www.agapebiblestudy.com/) explicitly identifies itself as a free Catholic Scripture resource. It offers in-depth Bible studies rooted in Catholic tradition, historical and archaeological context, Catechism references, Sunday readings, charts, maps, and study documents.

The [Institute of Catholic Culture](https://instituteofcatholicculture.org/) appears in current search results as a provider of free online adult Catholic education across theology, Scripture, philosophy, Church history, and related liberal-arts topics. Its homepage did not render in the sandbox browser, so the Resources page should use a conservative description: “Free Catholic adult-formation classes and lectures.”

## Apologetics, Sermons, and Media

[Catholic Answers](https://www.catholic.com/) provides searchable Catholic Q&A, articles, apologetics tracts, an encyclopedia, a Bible Navigator, podcasts, radio, and videos. It is suitable for a “Questions about the Faith” resource card.

[Word on Fire](https://www.wordonfire.org/) provides freely accessible Sunday sermons, Gospel reflections, videos, the Word on Fire Show, the Evangelization & Culture podcast, Rosary videos, and articles on Catholic faith and culture. It is suitable for both the podcast and formation sections.

## Final Curated Set

The page should prioritize a concise set rather than an exhaustive directory: USCCB Daily Readings, USCCB Bible, Vatican Catechism, Diocese of Rapid City, Agape Bible Study, Institute of Catholic Culture, Ascension Podcasts, Word on Fire, Catholic Answers, USCCB Basic Prayers, Vatican News, and West River Catholic.
